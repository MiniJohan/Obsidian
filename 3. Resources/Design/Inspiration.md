## Specific Things to Steal (Ethically)

- **Linear's focus ring** — outline offset, subtle but visible
- **Vercel's table row hover** — barely visible bg shift on dark
- **Things 3's bottom sheet pull handle** — the 36x4px rounded bar at top
- **Raycast's empty state** — small icon, short copy, single CTA
- **Spotify's now-playing bar** — persistent, minimal, tappable

## Notes on Using References

Studying these is useful. Copying them directly is not.
The goal is to understand *why* a decision works, then apply that reasoning — not the pixel values.

Questions to ask when looking at a reference:
- What problem does this solve?
- What did they choose not to include?
- Does this hold up on a 390px phone screen?
- Would I have made the same call?
