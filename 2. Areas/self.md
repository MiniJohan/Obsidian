# Self

> Not a productivity system. A place to not lose the thread.

---

## When stuck

Pick one. Don't overthink it.

- Build something tiny — a script, a component, a page. Output anything, even throwaway code.
- Open a tutorial on something you've been vaguely curious about and just start
- Go to [[0. Inbox]] and write down whatever half-formed thought is floating around
- Give VALE a weird task and see what breaks or surprises you
- Do more push-ups, or go outside for 10 minutes
- Pick something from **Next edges** below and spend 5 minutes on it
- Scroll through **Ideas & seeds** — is anything actually buildable right now?

## Next edges

Small, concrete things worth picking up. One at a time — don't stack them.

- Increase push-ups to 10, then 15 (already have the base)
- Read something non-technical for 10 minutes a day
- Write one short thing per week — a paragraph, a post, a note, anything
- Go outside briefly every day, even 5 minutes
- Learn one small technical thing weekly, write 3 sentences about it to lock it in

## Monthly review

5 minutes. Update in-place. No archive needed — just overwrite the notes each time.

1. What's actually going well right now?
2. What keeps draining me or blocking forward motion?
3. Did I pick up anything from Next edges? What happened?
4. Any ideas in the Inbox or Ideas section worth keeping or building?
5. What's one small thing I want to work on next month?
